### AWS Client VPN extends availability to four additional AWS Regions

- **Link**: [https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/07/aws-client-vpn-four-additional-regions/](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/07/aws-client-vpn-four-additional-regions/)
- **Published**: 2026-07-10 02:08:00
- **Fetched**: 2026-07-10 03:47:37

<p>AWS Client VPN is now available in four new regions: Canada West (Calgary), Mexico (Central) and two in Asia Pacific - New Zealand and Taipei. This fully managed service enables customers to securely connect their remote workforce to resources in AWS or on-premises networks.</p> 
<p>AWS Client VPN eliminates the need for hardware VPN appliances and complex operational management through its pay-as-you-go model. Organizations can easily manage and monitor VPN connections through a single console.<br /> To learn more about Client VPN:<br /> </p>
