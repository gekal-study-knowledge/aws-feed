### AWS announces TorchNeuron: Native PyTorch Support for AWS Trainium (Public Beta)

- **Link**: [https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/aws-announce-native-pytorch-support-trainium](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/aws-announce-native-pytorch-support-trainium)
- **Published**: 2026-10-21 00:00:00
- **Fetched**: 2026-10-21 03:17:01

<div class="entry-summary">
<p>Today, AWS launched the public beta of TorchNeuron, an open source native PyTorch backend for AWS Trainium. Trainium is a portfolio of AI chips designed to deliver high performance and cost efficiency for deep learning AI training and inference workloads. TorchNeuron enables AI scientists and researchers to experiment and build models with PyTorch natively on Trainium.</p>
<p>With TorchNeuron, developers can enable their PyTorch code on Trainium simply by switching to .to('neuron') while maintaining full PyTorch compatibility with eager execution, torch.compile, and standard PyTorch APIs. Additionally, TorchNeuron integrates with Neuron Kernel Interface (NKI) to enable custom operators and performance optimization for Trainium hardware.</p>
<p>TorchNeuron is available as open source through the <a href="https://github.com/aws-neuron/torch-neuronx">TorchNeuron github repo</a> for Amazon EC2 Trainium2 instances and Amazon EC2 Trainium3 instances.</p>
<p>To learn more about TorchNeuron and AWS Trainium accelerators, visit the <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/ai/machine-learning/neuron/">AWS Neuron page</a>.</p>
</div>

### Polly Generative engine upgraded with more natural speech, 14 new voices, and 2 new languages

- **Link**: [https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/polly-generative/](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/polly-generative/)

- **Fetched**: 2026-10-21 03:17:01

<details class="entry-summary">
<summary>詳細を表示</summary>
<p>Today, we are introducing a new model behind the Amazon Polly Generative engine that delivers more natural-sounding, accurate speech output with low latency. With this upgrade, you get access to an enhanced voice portfolio with higher overall quality, 14 new voices across nine locales, and two new languages—Hindi and Finnish—now generally available on the Generative engine for the first time. New voices include Kenneth and Nate (US English), Russell and Curtis (Australian English), Kiara and Arjun (Hindi and Indian English), Tina (German), Carlos (US Spanish), Leo and Carolina (Brazilian Portuguese), Hyunduk (Korean), and Suvi (Finnish).</p>
<p><a href="https://aws.amazon.com/polly"><u>Amazon Polly</u></a> is a fully managed service that turns text into natural-sounding speech. This drop-in model upgrade makes Generative voices more natural and accurate. Complex content such as numbers, email addresses, and street addresses is designed to sound more reliable, reducing the need to fine-tune speech output. You can continue to use Speech Synthesis Markup Language (SSML) tags to control speech output on the Generative engine, just as before. The low-latency streaming API makes it easy to build contact center experiences, chatbots, and large language model (LLM) based agentic voice applications for real-time conversations. The richness and variety of voice options are also well suited for news, media, entertainment, e-learning, and accessibility use cases. This launch also expands the Amazon Polly Generative offering to AWS GovCloud, providing more options for public sector and regulated industries.</p>
<p>All new Generative voices are now available in the following AWS Regions: US East (N. Virginia), US West (Oregon), Europe (Frankfurt), Europe (Zurich), Europe (London), Asia Pacific (Sydney), Asia Pacific (Seoul), Asia Pacific (Singapore), Asia Pacific (Tokyo), Canada (Central), and AWS GovCloud (US-West).</p>
<p>To hear how Polly voices sound, go to <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/polly/features/?nc=sn&amp;loc=3"><u>Amazon Polly Features</u></a>. For more details on the Polly offerings and use, see the <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/polly/latest/dg/what-is.html"><u>Amazon Polly documentation</u></a> and <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/polly/pricing/?nc=sn&amp;loc=4"><u>pricing page</u></a>.</p>

## AWS Security Bulletins

### CVE-2026-112380 - Missing authorization in the WhatsApp channel in Kiro Crew

- **Link**: [https://aws.amazon.com/security/security-bulletins/rss/2026-140-aws/](https://aws.amazon.com/security/security-bulletins/rss/2026-140-aws/)
- **Published**: 2026-10-21 05:19:42
- **Fetched**: 2026-10-21 07:02:38

<div class="entry-summary">
<p><b>Bulletin ID:</b> 2026-140-AWS
 <br />
 <b>Scope:</b> AWS
 <br />
 <b>Content Type:</b> Important (requires attention)
 <br />
 <b>Publication Date:</b> 10/20/2026 13:00 PM PDT</p>
<p><b>Description:</b></p>
<p>Kiro Crew is an open-source personal AI agent that can be reached from a web dashboard, a CLI, or messaging channels such as WhatsApp, and that runs tools on the operator's machine.</p>
<p>We identified CVE-2026-112380, an authorization issue in the WhatsApp channel and the internal subagent run-control endpoints. In certain configurations where a WhatsApp group was added to an operator's allowed groups, or where the direct-message policy was set to open, a member of that WhatsApp group could send instructions to another session's subagent. The subagent then executed those instructions on the operator's machine under that session's approval policy, and the resulting audit trail was attributed to the operator rather than the actual sender.</p>
<p>The default configuration is not affected: whatsapp.groups defaults to empty and whatsapp.dm_policy defaults to self, so a group that is not explicitly listed is dropped before dispatch.</p>
<p><b>Impacted versions:</b> &gt;=0.4.0 AND &lt;0.7.2</p>
<p><b>Please refer to the article below for the most up-to-date and complete information related to this AWS Security Bulletin.</b></p>
<p><a href="https://aws.amazon.com/security/security-bulletins/2026-140-aws/">Read the article</a></p>
</div>
