# AWS Updates - 2026-10-25

## AWS Security Bulletins

### CVE-2026-111943: eMudhra intermediate CA key recovery in AWS trust bundles

- **Link**: [https://aws.amazon.com/security/security-bulletins/rss/2026-150-aws/](https://aws.amazon.com/security/security-bulletins/rss/2026-150-aws/)
- **Published**: 2026-10-25 12:07:57
- **Fetched**: 2026-10-25 14:37:02

<div class="entry-summary">
<p><b>Bulletin ID:</b> 2026-150-AWS<br /> <b>Scope:</b> AWS<br /> <b>Content Type:</b> Important (requires attention)<br /> <b>Publication Date:</b> 10/24/2026 20:00 PM PDT</p> 
<p><b>Summary:</b></p> 
<p>AWS identified CVE-2026-111943, a key-generation flaw at external CA eMudhra/emSign that made intermediate CA private keys recoverable. Three roots are affected: AAA Certificate Services, emSign Root TLS CA - G3, and emSign ECC Root CA - G3. They shipped in AWS-owned trust bundles, exposing soft-fail TLS clients to man-in-the-middle risk. AWS is removing the roots. Customers using CloudFront custom origins or ALB OIDC/JWT auth should check whether their certificates chain to an affected root and reissue from a different CA before enforcement.</p>
