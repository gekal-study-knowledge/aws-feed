# AWS Updates - 2026-11-12

## AWS What's New

### AWS Quick apps are now available in Asia Pacific (Singapore)

- **Link**: [https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/aws-quick-apps-available-in-asia-pacific-singapore/](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/aws-quick-apps-available-in-asia-pacific-singapore/)
- **Published**: 2026-11-12 00:20:00
- **Fetched**: 2026-11-12 03:19:01

<div class="entry-summary">
<p>The AWS Quick desktop app for macOS and Windows and the Quick mobile app for iOS and Android are now available in the Asia Pacific (Singapore) Region. This launch builds on the recent <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/10/amazon-quick-now-available-in-the-aws-singapore-region/">availability of Quick in Singapore</a>.</p> 
<p>The desktop app works with your local files and connects to your calendar, email, and business apps. The mobile app lets you chat with Quick, review your activity feed, and open dashboards. Your conversations, context, and agents stay in sync across web, desktop, and mobile, so you can start an agent on your desktop, check on it from your phone, and let it keep running after you close your computer.&nbsp;</p> 
<p>To get started, download the desktop app from the <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/quick/download/">AWS Quick download page</a> or the mobile app from the <a href="https://apps.apple.com/us/app/amazon-quick/id1148226615">Apple App Store</a> or <a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.amazonaws.quicksight.mobile&amp;hl=en_US&amp;trk=e0dcca02-d7b6-48fd-a721-6f3538a9b485&amp;sc_channel=el" target="_blank">Google Play</a>. To learn more, see <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/quick/latest/userguide/amazon-quick-mobile.html">AWS Quick on mobile</a> in the AWS Quick User Guide and the <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/quick/">AWS Quick product page</a>.</p>
</div>
