# AWS Updates - 2026-11-20

## AWS What's New

### Amazon Aurora DSQL now supports multi-Region peering across continents

- **Link**: [https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/aurora-dsql-intercontinental-multi-region-clusters/](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/11/aurora-dsql-intercontinental-multi-region-clusters/)
- **Published**: 2026-11-20 00:56:00
- **Fetched**: 2026-11-20 03:48:46

<div class="entry-summary">
<p><a href="https://aws.amazon.com/rds/aurora/dsql/">Amazon Aurora DSQL</a> now lets you create multi-Region clusters whose Regions are on different continents, such as US East (N. Virginia) and Europe (Ireland). Until now, the Regions in a multi-Region cluster had to be in the same geographic Region set.</p> 
<p>With intercontinental multi-Region clusters, applications on two continents read and write to one strongly consistent database. If a Region becomes unavailable, committed writes are preserved and applications keep reading and writing in the other Region. You choose the two peer Regions and the witness Region. Place your database close to users on each continent, or separate failure domains as widely as your resilience needs require.</p> 
<p>Intercontinental multi-Region clusters are available in all <a href="https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/global-infrastructure/regional-product-services/">AWS Regions</a> where Aurora DSQL supports multi-Region clusters. To learn more, see <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/aurora-dsql/latest/userguide/what-is-aurora-dsql.html#aurora-dsql-multi-region-availability">Multi-Region cluster availability</a> in the Aurora DSQL User Guide.</p>
</div>

### PHP Foundation Security Releases – November 19, 2026

- **Link**: [https://aws.amazon.com/security/security-bulletins/rss/2026-190-aws/](https://aws.amazon.com/security/security-bulletins/rss/2026-190-aws/)
- **Published**: 2026-11-20 03:30:09
- **Fetched**: 2026-11-20 03:48:47

<div class="entry-summary">
<p><b>Bulletin ID:</b> 2026-190-AWS<br /> <b>Publication Date:</b> 11/19/2026 10:00 AM PST</p> 
<p><b>Description:</b></p> 
<p>AWS is aware of security releases that the PHP Foundation published on November 19, 2026. PHP versions 8.2.36, 8.3.37, 8.4.28, and 8.5.13 address 17 issues across the PHP 8.2, 8.3, 8.4, and 8.5 branches. The issues are in the following components:<br /> - The PHP engine<br /> - PHP-FPM and the PHP CGI binary<br /> - The SOAP, Phar, session, DOM, and date extensions<br /> - The HTTP and FTP stream wrappers<br /> - The PHP 8.5 RFC 3986 URI implementation</p> 
<p><b>Patching the affected software remains the only complete fix and should be the first priority.</b> We will update this bulletin as more information becomes available.</p>
