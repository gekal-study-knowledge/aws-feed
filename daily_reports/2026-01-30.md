# AWS Updates - 2026-01-30

## AWS What's New

### New Partner Revenue Measurement gives visibility into AWS service consumption

- **Link**: https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/01/new-partner-revenue-measurement
- **Published**: 2026-01-30

<p>Today, AWS announces the launch of Partner Revenue Measurement, a new capability that gives AWS Partners visibility into how their solutions impact AWS service consumption across partner-managed and customer-managed accounts.</p> 
<p>Partner Revenue Measurement allows Partners to better understand their AWS revenue impact and product consumption patterns. Partners can now tag AWS resources using the product code from their AWS Marketplace listing with tag key: aws-apn-id and tag value<i><i>:</i></i> pc:&lt;AWS Marketplace product-code&gt; to quantify and measure the AWS revenue impact of that solution.</p> 
<p>Partner Revenue Measurement is generally available in all commercial regions. To learn more about implementing Partner Revenue Measurement, review the <a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/PRM/latest/aws-prm-onboarding-guide/what-is-service.html">onboarding guide</a> for more information.</p>
