# AWS Updates - 2026-03-03

## AWS What's New

### Policy in Amazon Bedrock AgentCore is now generally available

- **Link**: https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/03/policy-amazon-bedrock-agentcore-generally-available/
- **Published**: 2026-03-03

<p>Policy in Amazon Bedrock&nbsp;AgentCore&nbsp;is now generally available, providing organizations with centralized, fine-grained controls for agent-tool interactions.&nbsp;Policy&nbsp;operates&nbsp;outside your&nbsp;agent&nbsp;code, enabling security,&nbsp;compliance, and operations teams to define&nbsp;tool&nbsp;access and&nbsp;input&nbsp;validation&nbsp;rules&nbsp;without&nbsp;modifying&nbsp;agent code.&nbsp;Teams can author policies using natural language that automatically converts to Cedar,&nbsp;the&nbsp;AWS&nbsp;open-source policy language.&nbsp;Policies&nbsp;are stored&nbsp;in&nbsp;a&nbsp;policy engine&nbsp;and&nbsp;attached&nbsp;to&nbsp;an&nbsp;AgentCore&nbsp;Gateway,&nbsp;which&nbsp;intercepts&nbsp;agent-tool&nbsp;traffic&nbsp;and&nbsp;evaluates each request&nbsp;against the policies&nbsp;before allowing or denying tool&nbsp;access.&nbsp;Policy&nbsp;helps&nbsp;ensure&nbsp;agents&nbsp;operate&nbsp;within defined parameters while&nbsp;maintaining&nbsp;organizational&nbsp;visibility and governance.</p> 
<p>Policy in AgentCore&nbsp;is available in&nbsp;<a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock-agentcore/latest/devguide/agentcore-regions.html">thirteen&nbsp;AWS&nbsp;Regions</a>:&nbsp;US East (N. Virginia), US East (Ohio), US West (Oregon), Asia Pacific (Mumbai), Asia Pacific (Seoul), Asia Pacific (Singapore), Asia Pacific (Sydney), Asia Pacific (Tokyo), Europe (Frankfurt), Europe (Ireland), Europe (London), Europe (Paris), and Europe (Stockholm).</p> 
<p>Learn more about&nbsp;Policy&nbsp;in AgentCore&nbsp;through the&nbsp;<a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock-agentcore/latest/devguide/policy.html">documentation</a>, and get started with the&nbsp;<a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/bedrock-agentcore/latest/devguide/agentcore-get-started-toolkit.html">AgentCore Starter Toolkit</a>.</p>
