# AWS Updates - 2026-03-18

## AWS What's New

### Amazon Connect expands agentic speech-to-speech voice experiences to the London (Europe) region and adds three new voices

- **Link**: https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/03/amazon-connect-london-europe-region/
- **Published**: 2026-03-18

<p>Amazon Connect now offers agentic speech-to-speech voice experiences in an&nbsp;additional&nbsp;AWS Region: Europe (London). Amazon Connect also adds three new speech-to-speech voices across US Spanish and UK English: Pedro (es-US), Amy (en-GB), and Brian (en-GB).</p> 
<p>Amazon&nbsp;Connect's&nbsp;agentic self-service capabilities enable AI agents to understand, reason, and&nbsp;take action&nbsp;across voice and messaging channels to automate routine and complex customer service tasks.&nbsp;Connect's&nbsp;agentic speech-to-speech voice AI agents understand not only what customers say but how they say it, adapting voice responses to match customer tone and sentiment while&nbsp;maintaining&nbsp;natural conversational pace. With these updates, you can deliver agentic speech-to-speech voice experiences to customers across a new region with a wider selection of voices.</p> 
<p>To learn more about this feature, see the Amazon Connect&nbsp;<a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/connect/latest/adminguide/what-is-amazon-connect.html">Administrator Guide</a>.&nbsp;To&nbsp;learn more about Amazon Connect, a complete AI-powered contact center solution delivering personalized customer experiences at scale, visit the&nbsp;Amazon Connect&nbsp;<a href="https://aws.amazon.com/connect/">website.</a></p>
