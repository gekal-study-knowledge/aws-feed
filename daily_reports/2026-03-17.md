# AWS Updates - 2026-03-17

## AWS What's New

### Amazon Connect voice AI agents now supports 13 new languages

- **Link**: https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/03/amazon-connect-voice-ai-agents-13-languages/
- **Published**: 2026-03-17

<p>Amazon Connect now supports 13 new languages for voice AI agents, bringing the total to 40 language locales.&nbsp;&nbsp;New languages include Arabic (Saudi Arabia), Czech, Danish, Dutch (Belgium), English (Ireland), English (New Zealand), English (Wales), German (Switzerland), Icelandic, Romanian, Spanish (Mexico), Turkish, and Welsh.</p> 
<p>Amazon&nbsp;Connect's&nbsp;agentic self-service capabilities enable AI agents to understand, reason, and&nbsp;take action&nbsp;across voice and digital channels to automate routine and complex customer service tasks&nbsp;across multiple languages.&nbsp;&nbsp;</p> 
<p>To learn more about this feature, see the Amazon Connect&nbsp;<a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/connect/latest/adminguide/what-is-amazon-connect.html">Administrator Guide</a>.&nbsp;To&nbsp;learn more about Amazon Connect,&nbsp;a&nbsp;complete AI-powered contact center solution delivering personalized customer experiences at scale, visit the&nbsp;Amazon Connect&nbsp;<a href="https://aws.amazon.com/connect/">website.</a></p>
