# AWS Updates - 2026-03-17

## AWS What's New

### Amazon Connect adds new generative text-to-speech voices and expands to new regions

- **Link**: https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/03/amazon-connect-adds-generative-text-to-speech-voices/
- **Published**: 2026-03-17

<p>Amazon Connect now offers generative text-to-speech voices in three&nbsp;additional&nbsp;AWS Regions: Europe (London), Asia Pacific (Seoul), and Asia Pacific (Sydney). Amazon Connect also expands support for nine new generative text-to-speech voices across US English, UK English, European French, German, and Italian: Tiffany (en-US), Amy (en-GB), Brian (en-GB), Ambre (fr-FR), Florian (fr-FR), Tina (de-DE), Lennart (de-DE), Beatrice (it-IT), and Lorenzo (it-IT).</p> 
<p>Amazon&nbsp;Connect's&nbsp;agentic self-service capabilities enable AI agents to understand, reason, and&nbsp;take action&nbsp;across voice and messaging channels to automate routine and complex customer service tasks.&nbsp;Connect's&nbsp;voice AI agents understand not only what customers say but how they say it, adapting voice responses to match customer tone and sentiment while&nbsp;maintaining&nbsp;natural conversational pace. With these updates, you can deliver natural, human-like voice AI experiences to a broader range of customers across more regions and languages.</p> 
<p>To learn more about this feature, see the Amazon Connect&nbsp;<a href="https://docs.aws.amazon.com/connect/latest/adminguide/what-is-amazon-connect.html">Administrator Guide</a>.&nbsp;To&nbsp;learn more about Amazon Connect, a complete AI-powered contact center solution delivering personalized customer experiences at scale, visit the&nbsp;Amazon Connect&nbsp;<a href="https://aws.amazon.com/connect/">website.</a></p>
